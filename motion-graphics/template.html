Diagnostic Science

Told you have Malaria and Typhoid at the exact same time?

🦟
Malaria
Blood parasite via mosquito bite
AND
🧫
Typhoid
Bacterial infection via food/water
CLINICAL REALITY
True co-infection is actually rare.
⚠️

The Widal Slide Test Trap

Rapid slide tests pick up antibodies from past infections months or years ago.

🔬

Past Exposure ≠ Active Illness

Having old antibodies does not mean you have acute typhoid bacteria in your gut today.

Why treating both blindly is dangerous

💊
Organ Overload
Heavy antimalarials plus broad antibiotics strain your liver and kidneys simultaneously.
🛡️
Antibiotic Resistance
Misusing antibiotics builds superbugs, rendering medicines useless when truly needed.
The Golden Rule
Test Before You Treat.
1
Ask for blood film microscopy for live parasites
2
Check a Full Blood Count (FBC) & Urine test
3
Never self-prescribe antibiotics for a fever
