Told you have Malaria AND Typhoid together?

🦟

Malaria Parasite

Spread by mosquito bites into your red blood cells

CAN YOU HAVE BOTH?
🧫

Typhoid Bacteria

Spread through contaminated food or drinking water

✨ The Clinical Truth
True co-infection is actually rare!
⚠️

The Widal Slide Test Trap

Quick slide tests detect immune antibodies from past exposure months or years ago.

🛡️

Old Antibodies ≠ Active Sickness

Having antibodies in your blood does not mean live typhoid bacteria are in your gut today.

Why taking both blindly is dangerous

💊
Liver & Kidney Strain
Processing heavy antimalarials AND strong antibiotics at once strains your vital filtration organs.
🧬
Antibiotic Resistance
Overusing antibiotics breeds resistant superbugs, making future infections harder to cure.
The Golden Patient Rule
Test Before You Treat!
1
Ask for Giemsa blood film microscopy for live parasites
2
Run a Full Blood Count (FBC) to check true infection signs
3
Never self-prescribe antibiotics for a fever
